Lakeside Retreat

Residential Design Lake Tahoe, California 2023

This lakeside vacation home balances luxury with rustic charm, creating a peaceful retreat for a family from San Francisco. Natural materials and a neutral palette let the stunning lake views take center stage while providing a comfortable and elegant space for relaxation and entertaining.

Creating a design that would work for both intimate family gatherings and larger entertaining events, while maintaining a cohesive connection to the natural surroundings was our main focus. We focused on versatile, comfortable furnishings arranged in conversational groupings, with materials that reference the surrounding nature. Large windows were left unobstructed to maximize views, while custom lighting creates ambiance for evening gatherings.

The result is a sophisticated yet comfortable retreat that the family uses year-round, with spaces that transition seamlessly from summer lake activities to cozy winter gatherings.

"JMC Studio created our dream lake house. Every detail is thoughtfully considered, and the space works perfectly for both quiet weekends and hosting friends and family. It truly feels like a sanctuary."

— Michael & Lauren Thompson, Homeowners
