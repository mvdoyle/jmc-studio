Willow Restaurant

Commercial Design Seattle, Washington 2021

Willow Restaurant is a farm-to-table dining establishment that celebrates the Pacific Northwest's bounty. Our design emphasizes natural materials and a connection to the surrounding landscape while creating intimate dining zones within the larger space.

Creating a cohesive dining experience in a challenging industrial space with high ceilings and exposed mechanical systems was the central challenge. We embraced the industrial elements by contrasting them with warm woods, natural stone, and lush greenery. Custom lighting installations create intimate zones throughout the space, while maintaining an open, airy feel.

The result is a popular dining destination that has been featured in several design publications for its innovative approach to restaurant design.

"Working with JMC Studio was the best decision we made for our restaurant. They created a space that perfectly communicates our brand and philosophy while providing a memorable experience for our guests."

— David Chen, Owner, Willow Restaurant
