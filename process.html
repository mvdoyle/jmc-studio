Our Six-Step Design Process

01

Initial Consultation

Every project begins with a meaningful conversation. We'll discuss your vision, lifestyle needs, and the scope of your project. This consultation allows us to understand your aesthetic preferences and practical requirements.

During this initial meeting, we'll walk through your space, discuss your goals, and begin to formulate ideas about how we can transform your environment to better suit your needs and reflect your personal style.

02

Project Development

Following the initial consultation, we'll create a detailed proposal outlining the scope of work, timeline, and investment. Once approved, we'll measure and photograph your space to begin the design planning process.

This phase sets the foundation for a successful project by establishing clear parameters and expectations. We'll establish communication protocols and begin assembling the team of professionals needed to bring your vision to life.

03

Design Concept

We'll develop a comprehensive design concept including space planning, furniture layouts, material selections, and custom elements. Through thoughtful presentations, we'll guide you through the design vision for your space.

This creative phase translates your needs and preferences into a cohesive design language. We'll present mood boards, sketches, and material samples to help you visualize the transformation, making adjustments based on your feedback until we've perfected the concept.

04

Implementation

Once the design is approved, we manage all aspects of procurement and implementation. We coordinate with contractors, workrooms, and vendors to ensure every detail of your project is executed to our exacting standards.

Our attention to detail shines during this phase as we source materials, place orders, and oversee production of custom pieces. We manage timelines, budgets, and quality control, keeping you informed throughout the process while handling the complexities of bringing your design to life.

05

Installation

The culmination of our process is the installation day, where all elements come together beautifully. We oversee every detail, from furniture placement to styling accessories, ensuring your vision is realized to perfection.

This exciting phase transforms your space in a carefully choreographed process. Our team manages delivery schedules, installation crews, and final placement of each element. We handle the logistics and troubleshooting so you can simply enjoy watching your new space take shape.

06

Reveal & Follow-up

We reveal your transformed space and walk through the details together. Our relationship doesn't end at installation—we follow up to ensure you're completely satisfied with your new environment.

This is the moment when you experience your reimagined space for the first time. We'll walk you through each area, explaining the thoughtful details and providing guidance on care and maintenance. Over the following weeks, we'll check in to address any questions or concerns, ensuring your continued delight with your new space.
